Built around one thing: uninterrupted study time.

Nirmal Library sits opposite the petrol pump on Surajpur Gali, near Bhagat Singh Chauraha in Bidhuna — a straightforward walk or ride for students across the tehsil. It's used every day by school and college students, and by candidates preparing for competitive and government exams, who need a room that stays cool, quiet, and connected for the whole day.

No shared desks with distractions, no unreliable Wi-Fi, no fighting for a plug point. Just a seat, held for you, for as long as your preparation needs it.
